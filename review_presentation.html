Context • Motivation

Problem Statement & Industry Motivation

The Limits of Traditional Signature & Kernel-Level Anti-Cheats

  • ⚠️
    The Epidemic: Sophisticated aimbots, triggerbots, and speedhacks undermine esports integrity and player retention.
  • ❌
    Signature Scanners Fail: Polymorphic code, Direct Memory Access (DMA) hardware, and external injectors evade hash checks.
  • 🔒
    Kernel Rootkit Concerns: Ring 0 anti-cheat drivers (e.g., Vanguard/EasyAntiCheat) introduce OS instability and privacy backdoors.
  • ✅
    The Behavioral Solution: Players cannot fake the underlying biomechanical laws of human motor movement and reaction latency.

Decision Matrix • Stage H

3-Tier Classification Matrix

Preventing False Bans Through Multi-Tier Confidence Thresholding

  • 🟢
    Tier 1 — Legitimate (P < 0.35): Normal human motor variance, natural micro-adjustments, human reaction latency.
  • 🟡
    Tier 2 — Suspicious (0.35 ≤ P < 0.75): Smurf accounts, sudden skill spikes, or subtle smoothing aimbots flagged for monitoring.
  • 🔴
    Tier 3 — Cheating (P ≥ 0.75): Inhuman snaps, zero-jitter crosshairs, instant triggerbot activations.
  • 🛡️
    Production Safety: No automatic account terminations on ambiguous data — high reliability policy.
